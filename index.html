The short version

Your photographs are processed on your phone and are never uploaded. When you translate, only the text that was recognised in the photo is sent to a translation service. No account is required. Your translation history stays on your device until you delete it.

Who we are: Image Translator is an Android application. Contact: lens56315@gmail.com.

What is processed on your device

  • Photographs — text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your phone using Google ML Kit. A compressed copy is saved only if you choose to keep the translation in your history.
  • On-device translation — when you have downloaded a language model, translation also runs entirely on your phone.
  • History, preferences and usage counters — stored in a local database inside the app sandbox.

What leaves your device, and when

If on-device translation is not available for your language pair, the recognised text is sent over HTTPS to our translation provider (MyMemory, api.mymemory.translated.net) so that it can be translated. The photograph itself is never sent.

  • Text is sent only when you start a translation, and only after you have agreed to it in the app.
  • No name, email, device identifier or location is attached — the app has no account system.
  • The translation provider may retain submitted text under its own policy. If you are translating confidential documents, use on-device translation instead.

Speech and text-to-speech

Voice features use your phone's speech recognition service. Depending on your device and its settings, speech recognition may be processed by Google on their servers; this is controlled by your device settings, not by this app. The microphone is used only while you hold an active listening session. Text-to-speech uses voices already installed on your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your photographs.
  • We do not create user accounts or ask for an email address.
  • We do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell data.
  • We do not send text in the background.

Retention and deletion

All app data lives in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete it at any time:

  • History → Clear history — deletes stored translations (favourites are kept unless you confirm full deletion).
  • Settings → Delete all history — removes all translations and stored images.
  • Settings → Reset app settings — returns preferences to defaults.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.

We hold no server-side copy of your translations, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details. Subscription status is verified through Google Play Billing.

Legal bases (GDPR, EU users)

  • Performance of a contract / legitimate interest — processing necessary to provide the translation you requested.
  • Consent — sending recognised text to a cloud translation provider. You are asked explicitly, and you can avoid it entirely by using on-device translation.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Because the app stores data locally, you can exercise most of these directly (see Retention and deletion). For data already transmitted to a translation provider, contact us and we will help you raise the request with that provider. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority.

Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS.
  • The app contains no API keys or secrets.
  • App data sits in Android's per-app sandbox.
  • On-device processing is used wherever technically possible.

No system is perfectly secure. Avoid translating highly sensitive documents with cloud translation enabled.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Material changes will be shown in the app before taking effect, with the updated date at the top of this policy.
