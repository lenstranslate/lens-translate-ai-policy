Machine translation is not a translator. A translation can read fluently and still be completely wrong. Fluency is not accuracy.

Translations are automated and can be wrong

Image Translator reads text with on-device machine learning and translates it with automated engines. These systems are statistical. They can misread characters, drop words, merge sentences, invent plausible-sounding phrases, mix languages, and mistranslate idioms, names, numbers, units, negations and technical terminology.

Always verify before you act

You are responsible for checking every translation against the original text, or against a qualified human translator, before relying on it — especially before signing, paying, travelling, taking medicine, giving instructions, or making any decision that matters.

Never use automated output for

  • Medical, pharmaceutical or health decisions
  • Legal, contractual, immigration or official documents
  • Financial or tax decisions
  • Safety, electrical, chemical, machinery or construction instructions
  • Emergency or crisis situations
  • Anything where a mistake could cause injury, death, financial loss or legal consequences

Text recognition limits

On-device text recognition in this version reliably reads Latin script only. Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and Cyrillic text may produce no text, partial text, or nonsense. Low light, blur, glare, handwriting, stylised fonts, curved surfaces and small text all increase the error rate.
